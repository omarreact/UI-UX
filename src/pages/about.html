What matters

Principles that
shape each decision.

These principles are built into the visual and interaction patterns across the whole showcase.

01

Clarity before complexity

Useful information comes first. Distinctive design should make the message easier to understand.

02

Purposeful personality

Color, type, motion, and imagery should express a recognizable character without getting in the way.

03

Accessibility by default

Responsive layouts, keyboard access, visible focus, and sensible interactions belong in the foundation.

04

Built to evolve

Modular components and reusable rules make the experience easier to grow and adapt.
